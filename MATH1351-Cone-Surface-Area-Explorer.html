Investigate the connection

Increase the number of base sides. What happens to the pyramid’s base and lateral faces? Select Show cone, then unfold the surface. Describe the two pieces that make up the cone’s net.

How does the radius of the circular sector compare with the cone’s slant height? How does its arc length compare with the circumference of the cone’s base?

A cone is not a polyhedron. Regular pyramids with more and more base sides approach a cone when their base radius and height stay fixed. For a pyramid, r is the distance from the base center to a vertex. In the cone view, ℓ labels the slant height; in a pyramid’s flat fan, L labels a lateral edge. The detached base is shown separately for clarity.

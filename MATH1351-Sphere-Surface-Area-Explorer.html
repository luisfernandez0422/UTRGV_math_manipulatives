Make a conjecture

Use the orange-peel model and the formula for the area of a circle with radius r to develop a formula for the surface area, S, of a sphere with radius r. Explain your reasoning.

Each circle has the same radius as the sphere. The animation reshapes curved patches as they flatten and illustrates the relationship approximately. For a perfect sphere, the surface area is exactly four times the area of one circle with the same radius.

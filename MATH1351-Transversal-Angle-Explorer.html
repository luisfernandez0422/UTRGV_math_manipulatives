Relationship summary
Congruent angles have equal measures. Supplementary angles do not have to be congruent; their measures add to 180°.
Angle Relationship Which ones? What is true?
Vertical Angles ∠1 & ∠4; ∠2 & ∠3; ∠5 & ∠8; ∠6 & ∠7 Always congruent. Opposite angles have equal measures.
Supplementary Angles (Linear Pairs) Examples: ∠1 & ∠2; ∠1 & ∠3; ∠2 & ∠4; ∠3 & ∠4. The same pattern occurs with ∠5–∠8. Always supplementary when they form a linear pair: m∠A + m∠B = 180°.
Interior Angles ∠3, ∠4, ∠5, ∠6 Describes location between lines m and n. They are not all congruent.
Exterior Angles ∠1, ∠2, ∠7, ∠8 Describes location outside lines m and n. They are not all congruent.
Alternate Interior Angles ∠3 & ∠6; ∠4 & ∠5 Congruent if m ∥ n.
Alternate Exterior Angles ∠1 & ∠8; ∠2 & ∠7 Congruent if m ∥ n.
Corresponding Angles ∠1 & ∠5; ∠2 & ∠6; ∠3 & ∠7; ∠4 & ∠8 Congruent if m ∥ n.
Same-Side Interior Angles ∠3 & ∠5; ∠4 & ∠6 Supplementary if m ∥ n: their measures add to 180°.
Same-Side Exterior Angles ∠1 & ∠7; ∠2 & ∠8 Supplementary if m ∥ n: their measures add to 180°.

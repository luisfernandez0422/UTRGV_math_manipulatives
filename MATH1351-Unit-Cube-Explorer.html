Think in layers

Each solid cube has side length 1 unit and volume 1 cubic unit. A complete rectangular prism has volume length × width × height. Empty spaces do not count toward the volume of your build.

Teaching prompts & interaction help
  • Hide the counter. Predict how many cubes fill one layer, then the whole prism.
  • Compare a 4 × 3 × 3 prism with a 6 × 3 × 2 prism.
  • In free building, make two different shapes with the same volume.
  • Drag with a mouse or one finger to orbit. Use zoom buttons on touchscreens.
  • With the model focused: arrow keys rotate; + and − zoom; Home restores the view.
  • Undo reverses a whole fill, clear, reset, dimension change, or building action. Reset all returns to an empty 4 × 3 × 3 space.

Sphere: Why There Is No Flat Net

A sphere separates into curved strips. Attempting to flatten them produces gaps and distortion.

This is an illustration of an attempted flattening, not an exact net. Even the separated strips must distort to lie completely flat; cutting alone does not remove the sphere’s curvature.
